About the dictionary

The late Colonel William Whitaker spent years compiling WORDS, a Latin dictionary and word-parsing program of around 39,000 entries drawn from classical, medieval and church Latin sources. The WORDS JSONisator turned his plain-text dictionary file into JSON, and this site builds dictionary forms, word types and English meanings from that file in your browser.

Good to know: spellings are Whitaker's, so there are no macrons and some words start with j where your textbook may use i (e.g. jacio for iacio). The verb sum, esse isn't in the data file, because WORDS handles it separately. More about limitations.
